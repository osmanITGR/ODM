Connect ODM to your browser

Three steps. Takes about a minute.

1

Open your extensions page

Browsers do not allow a program to open this page for you, so copy the address and paste it into the address bar.

chrome://extensions
2

Turn on “Developer mode”

The switch is in the top-right corner of that page.

3

Click “Load unpacked” and paste this folder

In the folder picker, press Ctrl + V and then Enter.

…
Last bit: click the ODM icon in your browser toolbar, paste the pairing token from ODM’s Settings, and press Save. The token is already on your clipboard if you just came from the installer.
